First Crore

Earned, Not Risked

Comes from your professional income, not the market. Invest it conservatively — this is capital you protect, not capital you gamble to hit an early target.

Second Crore

Where You Target Growth

Don't force your first crore to an ambitious number by 45 — that ambition belongs to your second crore, the one you've already learned how to build.

Remaining 99

Compounding, Not Saving

Once two crore are set aside as safety and liquidity, the rest is earned from the market — through sustained, disciplined compounding.

The conviction behind this compounding comes from the market's own long-run behaviour: Nifty has historically delivered close to 12% in years when nominal GDP grew 10–11%, and well-managed PMS strategies have added a further 3–4% in alpha on top of that.

The ₹1 Cr → ₹7 Cr → ₹100 Cr milestones are an illustrative math trail, built directly off Arya Alpha's own framework — 35 years of compounding, age 30 to 65, at an implied ~14% annually. They are shown to make the framework concrete, not as a projection, promise, or guarantee of returns.
