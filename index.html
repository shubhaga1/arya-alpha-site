02 · Our Philosophy

A good investment is not necessarily the one with the highest recent return.

It is the one that

  • Has a clear, repeatable investment philosophy
  • Is managed by a capable, aligned team
  • Has a portfolio we genuinely understand
  • Offers an attractive risk-reward proposition

And that also

  • Adds a meaningful, defined role to the portfolio
  • Fits the investor's goals, horizon and risk capacity
  • Complements — not duplicates — existing holdings
  • Can be explained in plain terms, not jargon

Our objective is not to own more products. It is to own the right exposures.

05 · Our Research Framework

Six dimensions, not just a factsheet

A factsheet is only the beginning of investment research. Every strategy is assessed across five dimensions, plus one question that matters more than any of them.

PeopleInvestment team, experience, track record, continuity, incentive alignment.
ProcessInvestment philosophy, research discipline, portfolio construction, repeatability.
PortfolioConcentration, sector and factor exposure, overlap, liquidity, position sizing.
PerformanceConsistency, attribution, benchmark comparison, drawdown behaviour.
RiskConcentration, volatility, drawdowns, liquidity, leverage, style/factor risk.
Portfolio FitWhat role does this investment play in the portfolio that already exists?

A strategy delivering 25% is not automatically better than one delivering 18%. Return tells us what happened — research helps us understand why, and whether it can happen again.

Public PMS/AIF leaderboards typically rank on short-term, pre-diligence absolute returns — a lens that favours the newest, smallest strategies rather than durable, repeatable ones. Our process is built to look past that lens.
